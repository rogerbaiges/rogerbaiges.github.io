# Portfolio Positioning

## Positioning Thesis

Roger Baiges Trilla is an unusually strong emerging AI research engineer: he investigates how advanced models fail and recover, engineers reliable systems under real constraints, and pursues ambitious independent multimodal and language-model projects. The portfolio should make research leaders, AI engineering teams, and technical founders see both depth and execution—not primarily a student profile.

## Governing Rules

- When several truthful interpretations exist, use the one that most strongly communicates Roger's ownership, technical difficulty, selectivity, results, and potential.
- Lead with evidence and outcomes rather than qualifications or caveats.
- Never invent a metric, rank, publication, production status, client, or business result.
- Do not minimize Roger's achievements or include self-deprecating context.
- Do not describe Roger primarily as a student.
- Use accessible language on the homepage; reserve specialized terminology for project pages.
- Do not mention that Roger has no publications.
- Do not use “passionate about AI,” “bridging the gap,” or “turning ideas into impact.”
- Do not use “I study internal representations” in the hero.
- Do not use the phrase “frozen-interface forgetting.”

## Claim Guardrails and Scope

- Prefer the pattern **approved verb + specific system or method + bounded setting + supported result**.
- Use first-person ownership only for work Roger personally owned. Preserve team attribution for collaborative projects; never imply solo delivery from a team result.
- CVC work may be called research and may use supported verbs such as “proposed” or “demonstrated,” but it must not be described as published, peer-reviewed, or accepted.
- Scope the CVC result precisely: activation-space repair restored **over 90% of lost performance in CLIP and DINO**. Do not turn this into a general accuracy claim. The Qwen result is an extension to retrieval **without rebuilding the index**, not evidence of production deployment.
- At Serimag, Roger pioneered the company's first trainable extraction prototype for its hardest banking document type through on-premises VLM fine-tuning and evaluation. Scope the result as **nearly 80% field-level automation at the required 98% accepted-field accuracy threshold**. Do not claim deployment, unnamed clients, revenue, cost savings, or broader business impact.
- “Four AI hackathon wins” is approved. Name the specific event and project when space permits; do not imply solo wins.
- EPFL must be described as **incoming** until enrollment status changes and is verified.
- Preserve the exact academic proof: UPC `9.19/10`, Top 5%, 7 Honors. Do not substitute older site figures.
- CatGPT + Patufet may be described as a 111M-parameter Catalan GPT-style model trained from scratch, with a custom tokenizer, base and instruction-tuned variants, and a 300M-token synthetic Catalan corpus. Do not claim state-of-the-art or production status.
- IpurdIAr may be described as an agentic multimodal tactical-football system using tracking data, inferred events, time-aligned commentary, and autonomous tool selection; it won the Barça Innovation Hack.
- LaIA may be described as a multimodal RAG assistant for Catalan public services spanning documents, images, speech, web retrieval, and form completion; it won the AINA Hack.
- Prefer externally inspectable evidence and direct artifact links. If scope is uncertain, use the narrower precise statement until it is verified.
- Organization names establish context, not institutional endorsement.

Avoid vague participation verbs when a stronger supported ownership verb is available. Do not strengthen a verb beyond the evidence.

## Selection Rubric

Score candidate hero proof, selected work, and recognition items from `0` to `2` on each dimension:

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ownership | Unclear | Meaningful contribution | Clear ownership or leadership |
| Technical difficulty | Routine | Substantial | Unusually ambitious or research-grade |
| Evidence strength | Assertion only | Inspectable artifact | Artifact plus bounded external or quantitative proof |
| Result | No outcome | Qualitative outcome | Specific supported result or selective win |
| Audience relevance | Peripheral | Relevant to one target | Strong across research, industry, or deep-tech audiences |
| Visual explainability | Hard to grasp | Explainable with copy | Clear system flow or truthful visual proof |
| Distinctiveness | Common profile signal | Useful differentiator | Memorable evidence few peers can show |

Prioritize the highest totals. An item with `0` for evidence strength or uncertain claim scope cannot lead the homepage, regardless of its total. When two items score similarly, prefer the one that adds a missing dimension to Roger's story rather than repeating evidence already visible.
