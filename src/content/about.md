## What I'm Working On

I'm currently doing my **bachelor's thesis at CVC**, where I study **catastrophic forgetting in OpenCLIP**. The project asks whether forgetting in a realistic adaptation setting is true erasure or a more structured internal drift, using **Sparse Autoencoders**, representation analysis, and steering interventions as tools, with a longer-term interest in what this means for more reliable adaptation and continual learning.

My broader research interests include:

- **Foundation models**
- **Multimodal learning**
- **Reliable machine learning**

## How I Like To Work

I like working at the boundary between research and systems. At **Serimag**, I worked on **high-variability Spanish financial documents**, helping drive a transition from OCR-plus-rules to trainable Vision-Language Models. A major part of that work was studying whether VLM probabilities were informative enough to support rejection decisions, so the system could give up coverage in exchange for much higher precision in sensitive banking contexts. At **CVC**, my work is more research-oriented, focused on failure modes, representation drift, and recovery in multimodal foundation models.
